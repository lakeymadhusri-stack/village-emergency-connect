About Rural Health Connect

Rural Health Connect is a student web project created to provide simple and accessible healthcare support for people living in rural areas.

The website brings important healthcare services, doctor information, appointments, medicine reminders, health camps and emergency contacts into one place.

Our goal is to make healthcare information easier to access through a simple digital platform.
